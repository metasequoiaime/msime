MSIME for macOS — bundled third-party notices
=====================================================

The input engine (crates/engine) is part of this project and is covered by the complete project license, GPL-3.0.txt. The dictionary and helpcode notices and the component license texts are included beside this file in the Licenses directory.

Components included by the macOS client include:

* The dictionary and helpcode data — the terms given in the dictionary and helpcode notices beside this file.
* zinnia — the handwriting recognizer in crates/engine is a port of it (Zinnia-LICENSE.txt). The Tegaki zh_CN handwriting model that recognizer reads is not in the package: the settings app downloads it the first time the handwriting panel opens, into resource-packs/handwriting under its data directory, with the model's own license (HandwritingModel-LICENSE.txt) beside it.
* The Japanese dictionary of Japanese mode (dict_japanese.dat), derived from the Mozc OSS dictionary — the terms in mozc_dictionary_oss_README.txt. It is not in the package: the settings app downloads it when Japanese is chosen, into resource-packs/japanese under its data directory, with that README beside it.
* symphonia 0.6 (symphonia, symphonia-core, symphonia-common, symphonia-metadata, symphonia-format-riff, symphonia-format-ogg, symphonia-codec-pcm, symphonia-codec-vorbis) and triple_buffer 9, linked unmodified into the input method for key sounds and background music — Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0.txt). Their source is the matching version on crates.io (https://crates.io/crates/<name>).
* rink-core 0.9, linked unmodified into the input engine for V-mode unit conversion — Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0.txt); its source is the matching version on crates.io (https://crates.io/crates/rink-core). The units database it embeds (definitions.units) is forked from the GNU Units database, Copyright Free Software Foundation, Inc., under the GNU General Public License version 3 or later (GPL-3.0.txt).
* The Chinese administrative divisions `@` mode offers, compiled into the input engine from modood/Administrative-divisions-of-China (https://github.com/modood/Administrative-divisions-of-China, commit c49d495b40ac73eb1a66f6eeae5f8fd10696f035), compiled by its author from the division codes published by the National Bureau of Statistics of China — WTFPL (Administrative-divisions-of-China-WTFPL.txt).
* The Korean Hanja table Korean mode converts a syllable with, compiled into the input engine from the single-syllable readings of libhangul's data/hanja/hanja.txt (https://github.com/libhangul/libhangul, commit 717409ce61524bb3d8426060a384822f21354c62), Copyright (c) 2005,2006 Choe Hwanjin — BSD 3-clause license (libhangul-hanja-BSD-3-Clause.txt).
* The Jyutping syllables and words of Cantonese mode, in the Cantonese dictionary (cantonese.db, downloaded when Cantonese is chosen into resource-packs/language-dictionaries under the settings app's data directory, with rime_cantonese_LICENSE.txt beside it), from jyut6ping3.chars.dict.yaml, jyut6ping3.words.dict.yaml and essay-cantonese.txt of rime-cantonese (https://github.com/rime/rime-cantonese, commit 259f0e48bba840c3a2e0d117539e96937f3d89bc) by the Cantonese Computational Linguistics Infrastructure Development Workgroup (CanCLID), in the Jyutping romanization of the Linguistic Society of Hong Kong — Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (rime-cantonese-CC-BY-4.0.txt, which also records the changes made). The ODbL-licensed jyut6ping3.maps.dict.yaml is not used.
* The bopomofo syllables and words of Zhuyin mode, in the Zhuyin dictionary (zhuyin.db, downloaded when Zhuyin is chosen into resource-packs/language-dictionaries under the settings app's data directory, with libchewing_data_LICENSE.txt beside it), from dict/chewing/tsi.csv and dict/chewing/word.csv of libchewing-data (https://github.com/chewing/libchewing-data, commit c44e81aef24b06f1509f19e1be54c99812d0c43f), Copyright (c) 2025 libchewing Core Team — GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1 or later (libchewing-data-LGPL-2.1.txt, which also gives where the unmodified source is published).
* The stroke orders of Stroke mode, in the Stroke dictionary (stroke.db, downloaded when Stroke is chosen into resource-packs/language-dictionaries under the settings app's data directory, with rime_stroke_LICENSE.txt beside it), from stroke.dict.yaml of rime-stroke (https://github.com/rime/rime-stroke, commit 1e8fff9b9494ddec23b0cbc526bcfd8171a6fd48) by 四季的風, 雪齋, Kunki Chou and 宋天, whose main table derives from the CNS11643 stroke data (數位發展部，CNS11643中文標準交換碼全字庫網站，https://www.cns11643.gov.tw) — GNU Lesser General Public License 3.0 (rime-stroke-LGPL-3.0.txt, which also gives where the unmodified source is published). Its candidates are ranked by the character frequencies of the dictionary data.
* vi 0.8.0 (https://crates.io/crates/vi), linked unmodified into the input engine for the Telex and VNI typing of Vietnamese mode, Copyright 2020 Hung Nguyen — MIT License (vi-MIT.txt).
* sherpa-onnx (Contents/Frameworks/libsherpa-onnx-c-api.dylib, loaded by the Contents/MacOS/msime-voice-local helper for on-device speech recognition) — Apache License 2.0 (sherpa-onnx-Apache-2.0.txt).
* ONNX Runtime, statically linked into the sherpa-onnx library as its inference backend — MIT License (onnxruntime-MIT.txt), with the notices of the components ONNX Runtime itself bundles in onnxruntime-ThirdPartyNotices.txt.
* Offline candidate glosses for French, Japanese, Spanish, Russian, German and Korean (offline-glosses/zh-*.db, when installed) — adapted from the English Wiktionary by Wiktionary contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0; the attribution, source revision and changes are in offline-glosses-NOTICE.txt beside those files.

The speech recognition models are not part of this application; the settings page downloads them on request, and each model directory carries its own license.

The source tree retains each upstream notice.
